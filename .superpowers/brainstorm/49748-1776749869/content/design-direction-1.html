Team SOUL redesign directions

Apple-inspired, but translated for an esports legacy site. Pick the direction that should drive the whole public surface: Home, Tournaments, Roster, Champions, and Community.

Current read

The site already has strong motion and complete structure, but almost every route is using the same dark glass vocabulary, the same chrome density, and the same loudness level. The redesign should create hierarchy by restraint: one dominant moment per page, cleaner product-style sections, and more confidence in whitespace.

Recommended

B. Titanium Editorial gives you the strongest Apple influence without making Team SOUL feel like a generic electronics clone. It keeps the black prestige, adds brighter editorial breathing room, and makes the BGIS / legacy moments feel more premium.

A. Monumental Cinema

Most dramatic

This keeps the site mostly dark and prestige-heavy, but removes the current clutter. It feels like Apple event-film energy pushed into esports.

  • Best if you want the homepage to feel like a premium launch trailer.
  • Tournaments and roster become cleaner but still black-first and atmospheric.
  • Remotion role: cinematic section transitions, soft parallax, stage-light reveals.

B. Titanium Editorial

Recommended

This is the strongest Apple-like route: bright premium surfaces, giant disciplined typography, and dark contrast reserved for championship moments.

  • Homepage feels closer to an Apple product story than a gaming landing page.
  • Tournaments and roster read like curated museum exhibits, not dashboards.
  • Remotion role: product-film reveals, slow typography lifts, ambient light sweeps.

C. Archive Museum

Most editorial

This is less Apple launch-page and more Apple editorial exhibit. It would make Team SOUL feel historic, curated, and almost luxury-brand archival.

  • Strongest if the goal is legacy and culture over hype and energy.
  • Could undersell the competitive sharpness if pushed too far.
  • Remotion role: gallery pacing, slow object reveals, timeline film loops.
